Hi! My name is Neel, and I do research on AI Safety. I'm currently doing a PhD at the CDT for <a href='https://www.responsiblenlp.org/'>Designing Responsible NLP</a> at the University of Edinburgh. My work focuses on the alignment of LLMs, by researching how this is typically done during post-training, why it is so brittle, and what this means for safety more broadly. AI is clearly a transformational technology, and I think that it is critical for us to get a better understanding of its guardrails and when they break.
